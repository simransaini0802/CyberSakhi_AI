SMALL HABITS, STRONGER SAFETY

Your online safety toolkit.

You don’t need to be a tech expert to pause and verify.

01

Pause on pressure

Scammers may create urgency, threaten account closure, or promise an unexpected reward. Take a breath before acting.

02

Protect secret codes

Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVVs, or recovery codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

03

Verify independently

Use the official app or type the known website address yourself. Don’t rely on contact details or links in a suspicious message.

04

Check the sender

Display names can be copied. Verify unusual requests using a trusted number or channel you already know.

05

Don’t install surprise apps

Avoid installing remote-access apps or files because an unknown caller or message asks you to.

06

Report suspected fraud

In India, use official government cybercrime reporting channels. If money was lost, contact your bank promptly and use the official cybercrime helpline 1930 where applicable.

Remember

A scanner can miss scams and flag legitimate messages. When money, identity, or account access is involved, verify with the organization through a trusted channel.
